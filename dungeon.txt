As you tumble in this dungeon, three robed
figures turn to face you. You immediately
recognise them as the Count, the Innkeeper and
the Stablehand. You also recognise someone else:
the severed head placed on an unholy altar between
the three men had previously sat on the shoulders
of your friend, Ernest Mulhoney. Of the rest of the
body there is no sign, but a large pot and three sets
of eating utensils on one corner of the room clearly
indicate what the three robed men have done. They, too
must be cannibals.

"The ritual must not be stopped!", the Count shouts!
"Continue, whatever happens you must continue!"

The other two men start, or maybe resume, mumbling in
something that sounds like a made-up language.
