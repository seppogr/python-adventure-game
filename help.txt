HELP FILE

You can use one or two words in commands, some verbs can be used either singularly
or together with a noun. Please note that all prepositions have been omitted
from the commands, so that for example

'ask about lamp' or 'go to plaza'
take the form
'ask lamp' or 'go plaza'

respectively. Hopefully this won't cause too much confusion. Below
is the comprehensive list of commands available in the game, with some
helpful notes. Happy gaming!


1-word:

'help'              ->  Brings out this help file.

'describe'          ->  Gives you a look around the room or area you are in.
                        Helpful in figuring out what to ask or pick up etc. Equals to
                        you looking about.

'chat'              ->  A casual greeting to the character in the room or area. Useful in figuring
                        out what further actions to take with the character.

'directions'        ->  You take a look around and see where to go next.

'inventory'         ->  What's in your bag at the moment.

'companion'         ->  Show your current companion (if any).

'commands'          ->  A quick help with all available verb commands used in the game. Nouns are
                        for you to figure out.

Context-specific one-word

The following commands produce a desired effect if certain in-game conditions are met:

'fish'               -> Catch a fish!

'read'               -> Read something.

'trade'              -> Some people will trade with you if you have something they want.


2-word:
Note: Don't write the '+' sign in the command. It's just here to clarify that these verb commands
work this way when there is a noun given as a second command word.

go + <place>        ->  Move from one place to another. If you cannot think of a next place to go,
                        have a brief look around with 'directions' command.

describe + <item>   ->  Describe an item more thoroughly. Can be important when you are trying to
    or <character>      find important clues.  This is not interaction, it is you looking at things.
                        For a general look into your whereabouts, use the single word 'describe' command.
                        If you wish to communicate, use 'ask <about>' command.

ask + <about>       ->  Ask another character about an item or place, or about some other character. If
                        you are unsure what to ask about, use 'chat' command. This is a specific interaction
                        with another character.

give + <item>       -> If you got a specific item that sombody wants, you can give it to them.

take + <item>       -> If there is somethiing in the room that you need or want, you can get it with this command.

request + <item>    -> Politely ask for an item in somebody else's possession. If they don't want to give it to you,
                       maybe they will simply 'trade' for it instead.
