THE STRANGE DISAPPEARANCE OF ERNEST MULHONEY

A while ago, I got a call from an acquientance, Ernest Mulhoney. "Hey Jim",
he started "Come over to Spruce Head and we'll take a grand tour of New England
in my brand new motor car."

"Sounds good", I answered, "Let me check my schedule for the week". I had a busy
couple of days coming up but towards the weekend my schedule seemed clear.

"How about I arrive on Thursday on the 11:34 bus?", I proposed.

Ernest thought about it for a moment, "Yeah, I guess I'll have the chance to use
that new fishing rod I got while I wait for you." So, the agreement was made and
we were to start our field trip on Thursday.

The next couple of days were filled with endless office tasks, but quite
miraculously, nothing new was piled up on top of the previous ones, and my desk
was clear by Wednesday night. In the morning I took the bus and arrived
propmptly on time in Spruce Head town square. However, of Ernest Mulhoney or his
brand new motor vehicle there was no trace. I waited for a while but it became
evident that Ernest was not going to be here anytime soon.

Perhaps it is time to figure out where he is.
