As you throw the symbol on the floor, something curious happens:

The symbol shatters into two pieces, and a strong surge of power pulses from the
exact place where the pieces of the symbol fell from each other. The power
clearly disrupts the ritual the three robed figures were conducting, and the
ground starts to shatter. Suddenly, a hole appears in the cavern roof! Sunlight
starts to stream into the dungeon and, as on cue, you feel it is now or never.
