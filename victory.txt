You make a dash for the hole by climbing on some boulders that fell. You barely
reach the churchyard through the hole. there are some muffled cries coming from
beyond but you press on. The cries stop eventually.

As you later talk with the smith, you both decide that perhaps it is too
difficult to explain the whole affair to the police and you decide to disappear
the way you came by the next bus. After all, there are now three more people
missing including the Count himself. It is likely the police will start pointing
fingers at travelers from out-of-town.

Later, when you take your seat in the near empty bus, you hear a quiet "meow"
coming from under your seat. The cat you first met in the attic, has also
decided it is time to change surroundings.
